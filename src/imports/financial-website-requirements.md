1. Legal & Compliance Requirements ⚖️

Financial services are sensitive, so these are essential.

Must-have pages:

Privacy Policy

Terms & Conditions

Disclaimer (important for financial advice)

Cookie Policy (if using analytics or cookies)

Financial disclaimers
Example:

“Information provided on this website is for educational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice.”

Clarify that results are not guaranteed.

Data protection

Follow General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) if you serve EU clients.

Secure handling of personal information.

2. Core Pages Your Site Should Have 📄
1. Homepage

Purpose: Quickly explain what you do.

Include:

Clear headline (what problem you solve)

Short intro about you

Main services

Testimonials

Call-to-action (Book a consultation)

Example structure:

Hero section

Services overview

Why choose you

Client testimonials

Contact/booking button

2. About Page

People want to know who they trust with money.

Include:

Your story

Qualifications

Certifications

Experience

Your philosophy about money and planning

3. Services Page

Clearly list your services, for example:

Personal financial planning

Debt strategy

Investment planning

Retirement planning

Budget coaching

Business financial consulting

Each service should have:

Who it is for

What problem it solves

What the client gets

Pricing (optional)

4. Pricing Page (optional but recommended)

Transparent pricing builds trust.

Example models:

Hourly consulting

Monthly advisory

One-time financial plan

5. Blog / Resources 🧠

Great for SEO and authority.

Examples:

How to create a monthly budget

Investment basics

Debt reduction strategies

Retirement planning tips

This builds credibility in Financial Planning.

6. Testimonials / Case Studies

Show results like:

“Helped reduce debt by 40%”

“Created a retirement plan in 3 months”

Social proof is extremely powerful.

7. Contact Page

Must include:

Contact form

Email

Phone

Optional: scheduling calendar

Tools like Calendly are commonly used.
